# Code of conduct

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free project.

Be constructive, assume good intent, respect differing experience levels, and
focus criticism on ideas and code. Harassment, discrimination, threats,
doxxing, and publication of private or sensitive information are not allowed.

Repository maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict
participation when necessary to protect the community. Report conduct concerns
privately to the repository owner rather than opening a public issue.
